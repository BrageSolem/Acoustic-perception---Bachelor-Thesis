This thesis was submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the 
degree of Bachelor of Engineering in Automation and Intelligent Systems at 
the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), June 2026.

\vspace{1em}

The work was carried out as a group project, with each author taking primary 
responsibility for distinct subsystems. \textbf{Mateusz Dabrowa} was responsible for the acoustic subsystem of the project, covering the complete signal path from the microphone array to acoustic direction estimation. This included the C-based STM32F413 firmware, DFSDM peripheral configuration, DMA-based audio buffering, and USB CDC communication. He also developed the Python-based acoustic processing pipeline, including signal preprocessing, feature extraction, GCC-PHAT based TDOA estimation, and DOA estimation. In addition, he designed and fabricated the custom PCBs and the microphone mount. \textbf{Andreas Jortveit} was responsible for the visual 
detection subsystem, including training and deploying the YOLOv8n object 
detection model, integrating the Hailo AI HAT+ accelerator on the Raspberry 
Pi 5, the camera pipeline and MQTT coordinate publishing, and the 3D design 
of the pan-tilt camera platform. \textbf{Brage Solem} was responsible for the 
machine learning sound classification pipeline, including the Convolutional 
Neural Network and decision-tree classifiers, feature extraction, dataset 
preparation, and the development of the web-based GUI and server. Overall 
system integration was a collaborative effort by all three authors.
